We are proud to announce that the **Sourcentis × Dennemeyer** collaboration was selected as one of the **five finalists of the Co/Start Awards 2026**, organized by the [House of Startups](https://www.linkedin.com/company/hostlu/) (HoST) in Luxembourg, in partnership with **Le Village by CA Luxembourg**.

## The Co/Start Awards

The Co/Start Awards celebrate the most successful collaborations between startups and established companies. They highlight how the agility and innovation of young companies can meet the expertise and scale of established players to create real value.

The awards evening took place on **October 1, 2026** at the House of Startups: each finalist presented their collaboration to the audience, who were invited to vote for the **Public Prize**.

## A collaboration for security

[**Dennemeyer**](https://www.dennemeyer.com/) is a leader in intellectual property management. **Sourcentis** develops open-source tools that help organizations better understand, map and secure their information systems.

Together, we combined our expertise to strengthen the security and resilience of Dennemeyer's digital infrastructure: an accurate map of the information system is the first step to identifying critical assets, understanding dependencies and managing compliance.

## Thank you!

A big thank you to the **Dennemeyer** team for their trust and for the quality of our collaboration, to the **House of Startups** and **Le Village by CA Luxembourg** for organizing the Co/Start Awards, and to everyone who came to support us and vote.

Being a finalist alongside such high-quality collaborations is a great recognition of the work accomplished and of the value open source can bring to businesses.
